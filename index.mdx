picobean is an AI-powered content marketing app at [app.picobean.ai](https://app.picobean.ai). You describe the creators you want in a single prompt, and picobean finds, filters, and ranks matching TikTok and Instagram accounts. From there you can save shortlists, run campaigns with email outreach, and track how creators and competitors perform.

## What you can do with picobean

- **AI account search**: Describe the creators or content you want in plain language and get a ranked results table.
- **Discover and Saved**: Browse creators and content, then organize favorites into folders with memos.
- **Campaigns**: Manage briefs, rosters, deals, product seeding, content review, and payment in one place. (Enterprise)
- **Email outreach**: Connect a Gmail or Outlook mailbox, send personalized emails from templates, and let the AI agent help with replies. (Enterprise)
- **Performance**: Monitor your accounts, influencers, and competitors, and measure campaign results.
- **Trends**: Explore viral content and meme formats with Trends and Meme Radar.

The interface is available in English and Korean. You can switch languages in [Settings](/account/settings).
