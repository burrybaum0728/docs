**Settings** controls your personal profile, app language, plugins, and notifications. It has two tabs: **Account** and **Usage & Billing**. This page covers the **Account** tab. For plans, credits, and invoices, see [Billing](/account/billing).

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Plugin Selection** | The feature sets that appear in your sidebar. |
| **Organization** | The workspace you belong to. It is set during onboarding. |
| **Notification preferences** | Email alerts for failed sends and weekly subscription digests. |

## Account tab

| Section | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Your Details** | View your **Name** and **Email**. Select **Change Settings** to edit your name. Email accounts also have **Change Password**. Your email address comes from your login provider and cannot be changed. |
| **Organization** | See your **Organization Name**. To manage members, see [Team and members](/account/team-and-members). |
| **Plugin Selection** | Choose which feature sets appear in the sidebar. |
| **Language** | Switch the whole app between **English** and **Korean**. |
| **Dark Mode** | Turn dark mode on or off. You can also do this from the toggle at the bottom of the sidebar. |
| **Mailbox Management** | Link or unlink your sending mailbox (Enterprise). See [Connect a mailbox](/email/connect-mailbox). |
| **Notification Preferences** | Turn email alerts on or off. |

## Choose plugins

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Plugin Selection">
    Go to **Settings** > **Account** and find the **Plugin Selection** card.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select plugins">
    Check the plugins you need: **Influencer Affiliate** (on by default) or **Trend Research**. See [Plans and plugins](/get-started/plans-and-plugins) for the tabs each plugin adds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select **Save**. The sidebar updates right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notification preferences

| Alert | When it is sent |
| --- | --- |
| **Send Failure Alarm** | A campaign email fails to send. |
| **Subscription Weekly Alerts** | A weekly digest of newly discovered creators. |

Select **Change Settings**, turn the alerts you want on or off, and then select **Save Settings**.

<Tip>
  Keep **Send Failure Alarm** turned on while campaigns are running. That way you notice bounced or failed outreach quickly.
</Tip>
