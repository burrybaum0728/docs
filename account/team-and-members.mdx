A picobean organization lets your whole team share searches, saved creators, and campaigns. Admins invite colleagues by email, grant or revoke admin privileges, and remove members who no longer need access. Member management lives in the **Account** tab of **Settings** and requires the Enterprise plan.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Organization** | The shared workspace that holds your team's campaigns, saved creators, and billing. |
| **Admin** | A member who can manage other members and billing. |
| **Member** | A regular user of the organization. |
| **Invitation link** | A one-time link sent by email that lets a new user join your organization. |

<Note>
  Inviting and managing members is available on the Enterprise plan. On other plans, the **Members** section is shown as unavailable.
</Note>

## View your team

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings">
    Select **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar. The **Account** tab opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Expand Members">
    Open the **Members** section. It shows the total number of members, how many are admins, and a table with each member's email, role, and join date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter the list">
    Filter by role to show only admins or only members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the invite dialog">
    In **Settings** > **Account**, select **Invite member**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter email addresses">
    Enter one or more email addresses and send the invitation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invitee joins">
    The invitee opens the link in the email, signs in or creates a picobean account, and is added to your organization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Invitation links can only be used once and expire after a while. If a link no longer works, send a new invitation.
</Warning>

## Change access for a member

Admins can manage each member from the actions menu in the **Members** table. Choose one action and select **Continue**:

| Action | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Change admin privileges** | Grants admin privileges to a member, or revokes them from an admin. You confirm the change before it applies. |
| **Remove member** | Removes the person from the organization after you confirm. |
| **Add member** | Opens the invite dialog. |

<Tip>
  Keep at least one admin in the organization so that someone can always manage members and billing.
</Tip>

## Switch organizations

If you belong to more than one organization, picobean may ask you to choose one when you sign in. Select the organization you want to work in on the **Select Organization** screen.
