The **Usage & Billing** tab in picobean **Settings** shows your current plan, your credit balance, your invoices, and a comparison of all plans. This page explains how credits are used, how to upgrade, and how to manage your subscription. For a full list of which features each plan includes, see [Plans and plugins](/get-started/plans-and-plugins).

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Plan** | Your organization's subscription level: Free, Basic, or Enterprise. |
| **Credits** | The monthly allowance used by table generation and daily monitoring. |
| **Monthly cap** | The number of credits your plan includes each month. |
| **Customer Portal** | The Stripe page where you update payment details and cancel a subscription. |

## View your current plan

Open **Settings** and select **Usage & Billing**. The **My Plan** card shows:

- Your current plan name with an **Active** badge.
- The subscription end date, if you have a paid subscription.
- Your remaining credits and monthly cap, shown as a fraction such as `150 / 300` and a progress bar.

## How credits are used

Select **View more** in the **My Plan** card to see **Credit costs**.

| Action | Credit cost |
| --- | --- |
| Table generation | 1 credit per 25 rows, rounded up. For example, 100 rows cost 4 credits. |
| Daily monitoring | 1 credit per day. |

## Compare plans

The **Compare our plans** section lists each plan with its included features. Features that need a higher plan are shown as locked.

| Plan | Price | Credits per month | Highlights |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | \$0 | 10 | Saved lists, folders, memos, translation. |
| **Basic** | \$60 | 300 | AI search, monitoring, CSV download, viral content dashboard. |
| **Enterprise** | \$499 | 3,000 | Campaigns, email outreach, mailbox management, team members, private DB, content upload. |

## Upgrade your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Usage & Billing">
    Go to **Settings** > **Usage & Billing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    In the plan comparison, select **Upgrade** on the plan you want. For some Enterprise purchases the button reads **Start payment**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete checkout">
    Enter your payment details in Stripe Checkout. Your new plan is active as soon as payment succeeds.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Manage your subscription

To update your payment method or cancel your subscription, select **Manage subscription** in the **Payment** section. This opens the Stripe Customer Portal.

<Note>
  Payments are handled by Stripe. picobean does not store your card details.
</Note>

## View invoices

The **Invoices** section lists past invoices. Use the month selector to choose a billing period.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Date** | Invoice date. |
| **Description** | Line item description. |
| **Status** | For example, **Paid**. |
| **Amount** | Invoice total. |
| **Invoice** | Link to the invoice PDF. |

If there are no invoices for the selected month, the table shows **No invoices for this month.**
