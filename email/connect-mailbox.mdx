picobean sends campaign emails from **your own mailbox**, so creators see a real sender and their replies come back to you. Before you send any outreach, link a Gmail or Microsoft Outlook account. This page explains how to connect a mailbox and how to check its status.

<Note>
  Mailbox management and email sending require the **Enterprise** plan.
</Note>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Linked mailbox** | The email account that picobean sends from and reads replies for. |
| **Sender** | The linked mailbox used for a campaign. Only the sender can resend a message. |
| **Mailbox** tab | The inbox in Campaign where all campaign conversations appear. |

## Link a mailbox

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the mailbox setup">
    Go to **Settings** and find mailbox management. Or, in **Campaign** > **Settings**, select **Link mailbox**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a provider">
    Select Gmail (Google) or **Microsoft Outlook**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree to the terms">
    Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. You cannot continue without accepting them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and grant permissions">
    You are redirected to the provider. Sign in and grant the requested permissions. When you return, picobean shows **Email Connected**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Provider Required** | Select an email provider before you continue. |
| **Terms Required** | Accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. |
| **Connection Error** / **We couldn't connect your email** | Try again. Make sure you granted every permission on the provider's consent screen. |
| **No mailbox is linked. Connect Gmail first.** | This appears in the agent settings. Link a mailbox before you turn on the agent. |

<Warning>
  If you revoke picobean's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings, scheduled sends and agent replies stop until you link the mailbox again.
</Warning>

## Mailbox status in campaigns

In campaign lists and conversations, mailbox and agent badges show the current state of each mailbox. For example, a badge can show that the agent is sending or that a send needs attention. Check these badges if emails are not going out as expected.

<Tip>
  Use a dedicated outreach address, for example [partnerships@yourbrand.com](mailto:partnerships@yourbrand.com), so that campaign replies stay separate from your personal inbox.
</Tip>
