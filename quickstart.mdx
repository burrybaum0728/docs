This guide takes you from sign-up to your first saved creator in about five minutes. You do not need a credit card to start, and you can preview many picobean features as a guest before you create an account.

## Prerequisites

- A web browser.
- A Google account or an email address.

<Note>
  Guest preview mode lets you try searches and browse results without signing in. Saving creators, sending emails, and other actions require an account.
</Note>

## Sign up or sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open picobean">
    Go to [app.picobean.ai](https://app.picobean.ai).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sign-in method">
    Select **Sign in with Google** or **Sign in with Email**. If you use email, follow the link or code sent to your inbox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name your organization">
    After your first sign-in, enter an **Organization name**. Your organization is the shared workspace for your team's searches, saved lists, and campaigns.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your interests">
    Select the topics or industries that match your marketing focus. picobean uses them to personalize recommendations.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Run your first search

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Search">
    In the sidebar, select **Search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe what you need">
    Choose **Account** and type a plain-language request, for example:

    ```text
    Korean beauty creators on TikTok with 50K to 200K followers who post skincare reviews
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check and submit">
    Select **Preview search** to see the criteria picobean extracted, adjust them if needed, then select **Submit search**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore results">
    Scan the results table, then select a row to open the account details with recent content and key metrics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Save a creator

<Steps>
  <Step title="Save">
    Select the bookmark icon on a creator and choose a folder, or create a new one.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a memo (optional)">
    Open **Saved**, find the creator, and add a memo for your team.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You now have a picobean account, an organization, and a creator saved to a folder.
