**Saved** is where creators and posts end up after you save them from Search or Discover. You can sort them into folders, filter lists, and add memos that your whole team can see. Your saved creators are then ready for campaign outreach. This page explains how to organize and use them.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Account / Content** | The two Saved tabs: saved creators, or saved posts. |
| **Folder** | A named list of creators, for example "Q3 skincare shortlist". |
| **Data source** | Where the Account list comes from: **Saved**, **Subscription**, or **Private DB**. |
| **Memo** | A note on a creator that your team shares. |

## Data sources

| Source | Contents | Plan |

## Organize creators

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Saved">
    Select **Saved** in the sidebar. Choose the **Account** tab or the **Content** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create folders">
    In the left panel, select **New Folder**. Right-click a folder, or long-press it, to **Rename folder** or **Delete folder**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add creators to folders">
    Select creators in the table, then choose **Add to Saved Folder** or **New Folder** in the action bar. To take creators out of a folder, select them and choose **Remove from list**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter and sort">
    Filter by platform, country, category, followers, engagement rate, gender, age range, contact info, sponsorship, TikTok Shop, Amazon Storefront, and excluded brands or competitors. Sort by **Recommended**, **Followers**, **Engagement**, or **Last sponsored**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add memos">
    Open a creator's details panel and go to the **Memo** tab. Record outreach history, pricing, or notes on content fit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Unsaving a creator with the bookmark icon removes them from **every** folder. Deleting a folder only removes the folder. The creators in it stay in Saved.
</Warning>

## Export and upload

| Action | How | Plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Export to CSV** | Select the **Download** icon. The export follows your active filters and selection. | Basic and above |
| **Upload creators** | Select **Upload**, then add a CSV or Excel file of creator handles. picobean collects their data and adds the matches to your Private DB. | Enterprise |
