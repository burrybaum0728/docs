**Discover** lets you browse picobean's creator database directly. Search answers a specific prompt with an agent. Discover is for open-ended exploration: you can filter, sort, and scan creators or their content to build a shortlist. This page explains the views, filters, and actions in Discover.

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Creators / Content** | The two Discover views: creator accounts, or individual posts. |
| **Filter chip** | An active filter shown above the results. Select its **x** to remove it. |
| **Hide** | Move an account to the end of the list and record the reason in its memo. |
| **Viral rate** | How far a post performs above the creator's usual level. |

## Explore creators

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Discover">
    Select **Discover** in the sidebar. Use the segmented control under the heading to switch between **Creators** and **Content**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search or browse">
    Type a prompt in the AI search bar, such as "micro skincare creators with TikTok Shop", and select **AI search**. Or skip the prompt and apply filters to the full database.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply filters">
    Select **Filters** to open the filter panel. Active filters appear as chips above the results. Select **Clear all** to reset them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort and switch views">
    Sort the results, and switch between **Card view** and **Table view**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or hide">
    Hover over a creator and select **Add to Saved Folder**, or choose **New Folder** to create one. In selection mode, use the action bar to **Save**, **Add to Saved Folder**, or **Hide** several accounts at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filters

| Filter group | Options |
| --- | --- |
| **Platforms** | TikTok, Instagram |
| **Countries** | Korea, the US, Southeast Asia, and more |
| **Categories / Sub-categories** | Beauty, fashion, food, tech, and more |
| **Followers** | Nano, Micro, Mid-tier, Macro, Mega, or a custom minimum and maximum |
| **Engagement rate** | 0–2%, 2–5%, 5–10%, or 10%+ |
| **Contact** | **Verified accounts only**, **Has email**, **Has contact** |
| **Creator profile** | Estimated gender, age range, primary content language, and last upload date |
| **Collaboration** | Sponsorship history, TikTok Shop, Amazon Storefront, and options to exclude competitor collaborations or sponsored brands |

## Sort options

| View | Sort by |
| --- | --- |
| **Creators** | **Recommended**, **Followers**, **Engagement**, **Last sponsored** |
| **Content** | **Viral rate**, **Views**, **Most recent**, **Saves**. You can also search content by hashtag or account handle. |

<Info>
  Hidden accounts stay saved and can be unhidden later. The hide reason is recorded in the account memo, so your team can see why an account was skipped.
</Info>

## Export to CSV

<Note>
  CSV download requires a **Basic** plan or higher.
</Note>

Select the **Download** icon in the toolbar. The export includes the visible or selected results, up to 1,000 rows in table view.
