The picobean **Performance Dashboard** shows how your own social accounts, the influencers you work with, and your competitors are growing over time. This page explains how to add accounts, choose a date range, read the core metrics and growth charts, compare competitors, and export a PDF report.

## Prerequisites

- A picobean account signed in at [app.picobean.ai](https://app.picobean.ai).
- At least one social account handle to monitor (yours, an influencer's, or a competitor's).

<Note>
  Some dashboard features depend on your plan. Views-over-time and viral content views require **Basic** or higher. See [Plans and plugins](/get-started/plans-and-plugins).
</Note>

## Key concepts

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **My account** | Your own brand account that you monitor. |
| **Influencer** | A creator account you track, for example a campaign partner. |
| **Competitor** | A rival brand or creator account you add for comparison. |
| **Core metrics** | Summary cards for followers, contents, views, likes, bookmarks, shares, and comments. |
| **Collecting** | Shown while picobean gathers enough history for an account. |
| **Last fetched** | When picobean last updated the data for the selected accounts. |

## Open the dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the dashboard">
    In the left sidebar, open **Performance Dashboard**. The page opens on the **Account** tab, titled **Account performance** ("Core account metrics and competitor insights at a glance").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select accounts">
    Use the account checkboxes at the top of the page to choose which monitored accounts the charts include.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check data freshness">
    Look at **Last fetched** to see when the data was last updated.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Add or manage monitored accounts

Select **Manage account** to open the account manager. It has three tabs: **My account**, **Competitor**, and **Influencer**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the account type">
    Open the tab that matches the account you want to add: **My account**, **Competitor**, or **Influencer**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add accounts">
    Add a single handle, add several at once in bulk, or import a CSV file. {/* TODO: verify the exact CSV column format */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove accounts you no longer need">
    Delete an account from the list to stop tracking it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Deleting a monitored account removes it from your dashboard charts and comparisons. Re-adding it later may require picobean to collect data again.
</Warning>

<Info>
  Newly added accounts show **Collecting** at first. The week-over-week percentage appears once 14 days (7 + 7) of data are collected. Days that are still accumulating appear faded.
</Info>

## Choose a date range

Open the **Date range** picker and choose a preset:

| Preset | Range |
| --- | --- |
| **Last 7 Days** | The previous 7 days |
| **Last 14 Days** | The previous 14 days |
| **Last 30 Days** | The previous 30 days |
| **Last 90 Days** | The previous 90 days |
| **All Time** | All collected data |
| **Custom** | Pick start and end dates in the calendar, then select **Apply** |

All cards and charts update to the selected range. Comparisons use the **Current Period** against the **Previous Period** of the same length (for 7 days, **This week** against **Previous week**).

## Read the core metrics

The **Core metrics** section summarizes the selected accounts:

| Card | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Followers** | Follower count and net change in the period |
| **Contents** | Number of uploads published |
| **Views** | Total views on content uploaded in the period |
| **Likes** | Total likes on content uploaded in the period |
| **Bookmarks** | Total bookmarks on content uploaded in the period |
| **Shares** | Total shares on content uploaded in the period |
| **Comments** | Total comments on content uploaded in the period |

Each card includes a sparkline and the change **vs Prev. Period**. Hover over a card for the exact totals.

<Tip>
  Views, likes, bookmarks, shares, and comments are counted on content **uploaded on each date** in the range, not on all activity that happened in the range. A post published before the range does not add to it.
</Tip>

## Analyze growth charts

- **Account growth**: plots **Followers** and **Contents** over time. Switch between **Daily** and **Cumulative** values.
- **Engagement growth**: plots **Views**, **Likes**, **Bookmarks**, **Shares**, and **Comments** for content uploaded on each date.

Each chart has a ranking table that lists accounts with **Total**, **Gain**, and **Gain %** versus the previous period.

## Compare competitors

Turn on **Compare competitors** to overlay competitor accounts on the charts and open the **Competitor comparison analysis**. Sort the table by **Ranking**, **Account**, or **Gain %** to see who is growing fastest. To add another rival, select **Add competitor**.

## Export a PDF report

Select **Download PDF** to export the current view, including the selected range, cards, and charts. If you see **PDF export failed**, try again, or use your browser's Print to PDF.

## Result

You can see how your accounts, influencers, and competitors are performing for any period and share the results as a PDF.
