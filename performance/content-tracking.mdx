The **Content** tab of the picobean Performance Dashboard lists individual posts from the accounts you monitor, so you can see which videos drive views and engagement. This page also covers **Trend Analytics Insight**, where picobean groups content about a topic into themes and rates each one.

## Prerequisites

- Monitored accounts added in **Manage account**. See [Performance Dashboard](/performance/dashboard).

<Note>
  Viral content views require **Basic** or higher. Content upload is available on **Enterprise**.
</Note>

## Browse posts on the Content tab

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content tab">
    Go to **Performance Dashboard** and select the **Content** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sources">
    Under **Select account**, choose one or more sources: **My account**, **My influencer**, or **Competitor**. At least one source must stay selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the date range">
    Use **Date range** to limit posts to a period, for example **Last 30 Days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort the table">
    Select a column header to sort, for example by **View #** to find top posts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Content table columns

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Preview** | Thumbnail of the post |
| **Title** | Caption or title |
| **Account** | The account that posted it |
| **Detected product** | Product picobean detected in the content |
| **Upload date** | When the post was published |
| **View #** | Views |
| **Like #** | Likes |
| **ER** | Engagement rate |
| **Comments** | Comments |
| **Shares** | Shares |
| **Reposts** | Reposts |
| **Bookmark** | Bookmarks |

### View a single post's performance

Select a row to expand it. The expanded row shows a chart of that post's metrics over time, so you can see whether a video is still growing or has peaked. Open the post details to see more, and use **Download PDF** there to export them.

<Tip>
  Filter to **My influencer** after a campaign goes live to compare partner posts side by side, then use [Campaign metrics](/performance/campaign-metrics) to review the campaign as a whole.
</Tip>

## Analyze themes with Trend Analytics Insight

Trend Analytics Insight lets you "Analyze trend content and account trends by source." You describe a topic, and picobean deploys an agent that finds related themes and rates each as good, bad, or neutral.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a topic">
    Select **Create Topic**. On the **Register new** tab, fill in **Describe topic** and optionally your **Brand name**. Add keywords for **Discover** (for example, "skincare routine, glass skin"), **Influencer** (for example, "K-beauty reviewers"), and **Hashtag** (for example, "#kbeauty").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the agent">
    picobean shows **Deploying agent**, then **Discovering themes**, then **Fetching TikTok content**. Fresh content usually appears within 2 to 4 minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review themes">
    In **Themes**, filter by **All**, **Good**, **Bad**, **Neutral**, or **Trending**, or use **Search themes**. Each theme shows a **Rating**, **Sentiment**, and **Occurrences**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the evidence">
    Switch to **Content evidence** or **Account evidence** to see matching posts and creators. Sort by **Relevance**, **Newest**, **Views**, **Followers**, or **Mentions**, then select **Open content** or **Open account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can register up to 2 topics. Manage them on the **Registered topics** tab and select **Remove** to delete one. If the agent reports "No fresh trends found", try a broader topic.

### Read a theme report

Open a theme to see its **Theme analysis report**: an **AI Summary**, a **Theme explanation**, **Views Over Time**, and **Upload Trend**. Use **View related content** or **View accounts** to jump to the evidence.

## Result

You know which individual posts perform best across your accounts, influencers, and competitors, and which themes are working for your topic.
