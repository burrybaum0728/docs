You create a campaign with a step-by-step wizard that collects your brief, targeting criteria, templates, and budget. After creation, you can add creators through AI search or the Discover panel, then move into outreach.

<Note>
  Campaigns are available on the **Enterprise** plan.
</Note>

## Prerequisites

- You are on the Enterprise plan.
- Your workspace has at least one connected mailbox if you plan to send outreach emails.

## Launch the wizard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Campaigns">
    Click **Campaign** in the sidebar, then click **Create campaign**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Walk through the steps">
    The wizard has five steps. You must complete each step before continuing. Press **Enter** or click **Continue** to move forward. Click **Back** to edit a previous step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Step 1: Campaign

| Field | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Title | Yes | Campaign name. Example: "Summer Glow Seeding". |
| Description | No | Short summary used by the picobean agent to find matching influencers. |
| Product | Yes | At least one product name. You can add multiple products with **Add product** and remove them with **Remove product**. |
| Start date | Yes | When the campaign begins. |
| End date | No | Optional campaign deadline. |

## Step 2: Setup

Choose how the campaign runs.

| Field | Required | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign type | Yes | **Unpaid seeding** (gifted) or **Paid seeding** (fee-based). |
| Campaign purpose | Yes | **Views** or **Conversion**. Used to recommend creators that match the goal. |
| Target budget | Required for paid | Total budget in USD, KRW, or JPY. Unpaid campaigns set this to 0 automatically. |
| Target influencer number | Yes | Choose 50, 100, 200, 300, or enter a custom number. |

## Step 3: Target

Pick channels and countries.

| Field | Required | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target channel | Yes | Choose **TikTok** or **Instagram**. YouTube is shown as **Coming soon**. |
| Target country | Yes | Choose one or more from the featured list, or search and select from the full country list. Featured options include United States, Japan, South Korea, GCC countries, European countries, LATAM countries, MENA countries, and CIS countries. |

## Step 4: Target influencer

Define the creator profile.

| Field | Required | Options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Follower number | Yes | Select one or more ranges: 1K–10K, 10K–50K, 50K–100K, 100K–500K, 500K+. |
| TikTok Shop only | Yes | **Yes** to search only for influencers with TikTok Shop sales history, or **No** for all. |
| Race & culture | No | Optional multi-select: East Asian, Southeast Asian, South Asian, Middle Eastern, White, Dark skin, Hispanic / Latino, Mixed. |
| Language | No | Optional multi-select: English, Korean, Japanese, Spanish, Arabic, Russian. |
| Content description | No | Describe the content style or topics you want, such as "Beauty and skincare routines, GRWM, product unboxing". The AI uses this to recommend creators. |

## Step 5: Templates

Optionally assign saved templates that the campaign will use for outreach and follow-up.

| Template type | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| First send | The initial outreach email to creators. |
| Reply | Templates for responding to creator replies. |
| Campaign guide | The content guideline sent after a deal is agreed. |
| Product shipping | Shipping notice sent after an address is collected. |

Only templates you have already saved in **Templates** appear here. If no templates are saved, you can add them later from the Templates page. Choose **None** for any type you do not want to assign.

## Create or defer

At the final step, click **Create campaign** to save the campaign. Behind the scenes, the wizard combines your criteria into a search prompt. By default, it defers influencer recommendations so you can run them later from the campaign's Recommendations tab.

If you want recommendations immediately, you can run them after creation from the campaign Overview.

## After creation: add creators

Once the campaign exists, open it and go to the **Target list** or **Recommendations** tab. You have two ways to populate the roster:

- **AI search**: Describe the creators you want in natural language. The agent parses your prompt into criteria and returns ranked results.
- **Discover panel**: Browse creators already indexed in picobean and add them directly.

To add many creators at once, see [Roster and deals](/campaigns/roster-and-deals).
