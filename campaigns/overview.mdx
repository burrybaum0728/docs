A picobean campaign organizes your end-to-end influencer collaboration from brief to payout. Each campaign has a brief, a target roster of creators, a set of email templates, and tabs that track progress across outreach, negotiation, shipping, content review, and payment.

<Note>
  Campaigns are available on the **Enterprise** plan. Basic and Free plans do not see the Campaign menu item.
</Note>

## Campaign list page

Open **Campaign** from the sidebar to see every campaign in your workspace.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Campaign | Title, description preview, and creation date. |
| Status | Current campaign state, such as Draft or Active. |
| Type | Unpaid seeding or Paid seeding. |
| Progress | Visual bar showing how far the roster has moved through the funnel. |
| Due | Campaign end date, if set. |
| Actions | Open the campaign detail view. |

Click a campaign title to open its **Overview** tab.

## Overview tab layout

The campaign detail page opens on the Overview tab. It is split into cards:

- **Brief**: Editable campaign name, description, products, dates, budget, target channel, and target country.
- **To do now**: Open tasks that need a human, such as AI drafts waiting for approval or inbound replies that need an answer.
- **Campaign progress**: The 7-stage funnel showing how many creators are at each stage.
- **Seeding goal progress**: Percentage of the target creator count that has confirmed participation or progressed further.
- **Budget**: Total budget, allocated amount, remaining, and a list of confirmed deals.
- **Assigned templates**: Which first send, reply, campaign guide, and product shipping templates are assigned.

Press **Edit** on the Brief card to change fields. Press **Save** to commit, or **Cancel** to discard.

## Campaign progress funnel

The funnel shows how creators move from the target list to published content. Each stage is exclusive: a creator only counts in the highest stage they have reached.

| Stage | UI label | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target List | Target List | Added to the roster but outreach has not been sent. |
| Sent | Sent (Email / DM) | First outreach email or DM was sent. |
| Negotiating | Negotiating | The creator replied and a conversation is in progress. Also includes guide sent, draft submitted, or revision requested. |
| Confirmed | Confirmed | Deal is agreed (for paid) or participation is confirmed (for unpaid). |
| Package Sent | Package sent | Product has been shipped to the creator. |
| Content Confirmed | Content confirmed | The draft content passed review and is approved to post. |
| Uploaded | Uploaded | The creator published the content and picobean matched it to the campaign. |

The funnel also shows conversion rates between stages as percentages.

Click **View recommendations** to open the target list and add more creators.

## Roster lifecycle stages

Each creator on the roster has one visible stage. The stage is derived from a combination of the stored roster status and the deal negotiation state.

| Stage | Badge color | Icon | When to use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Prospect | Slate gray | Minus | No outreach sent yet. |
| Contacted | Sky blue | Single check | Outreach was sent. |
| Negotiating | Amber | Clock | Conversation is ongoing. |
| Confirmed | Lime green | Double check | Deal agreed or participation confirmed. |
| Product sent | Sky blue | Single check | Package shipped. |
| Posted | Lime green | Double check | Content uploaded and matched. |
| Not participating | Rose red | X | Creator declined or was marked as declined. |

Click a creator's status chip to open a popover and change the stage by hand. Picking **Negotiating** or **Confirmed** updates the deal state; picking **Product sent** or **Posted** updates the roster status. When you move a creator from **Not participating** straight to **Product sent** or **Posted**, a confirmation dialog warns you because it will treat them as participating again.

## Understand the "To do now" card

This card shows items that need your attention.

| Section | What appears |
| --- | --- |
| Review AI draft | Agent-generated replies that are pending your approval or failed to send. |
| Needs reply | Inbound replies the classifier recommends answering manually. |
| Send guide | Creators who agreed and are waiting for the campaign guide. |
| Request address | Confirmed creators whose shipping address has not been collected. |

Each item shows the creator name, email, and a one-line summary of the needed action. Click **Review** to open the draft in the Mailbox, or **Handle in Mailbox** to jump into the conversation.
