Meme Radar groups viral TikTok content into meme formats that many creators are replicating. Each trend card contains videos that share the same sound and the same format. This lets you quickly understand what is popular and why, without watching dozens of unrelated clips.

<Note>
  Meme Radar requires the **Trend Research** plugin. If you do not see it in your sidebar, turn on **Trend Research** under **Plugin Selection** in [Settings](/account/settings).
</Note>

## What you will learn

- How Meme Radar discovers and groups trends
- How to filter by category and language
- How to read trend cards and video breakdowns

## Prerequisites

- A picobean account with the **Trend Research** plugin enabled

## How trends are grouped

Meme Radar uses the same audio track (music ID) as the grouping signal. When multiple creators reuse the same sound within the last 30 days, the system clusters those videos and confirms the format with vision analysis. Every video inside a trend card is verified to use the same sound and follow the same format.

Trends are refreshed weekly. A small number of editor-picked trends may also appear at the top.

## Browse and filter trends

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Meme Radar">
    Click **Meme Radar** in the sidebar. The page loads in your default language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch language">
    Use the **KR / EN** toggle in the header to switch between Korean and English. This reloads the page for the selected language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by category">
    Click a category chip to narrow the list:

    - **Dance challenge**
    - **Lip-sync & skit**
    - **Sound meme**
    - **Transition**
    - **Template**
    - **Other**

    Select the same chip again to return to **All**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a trend">
    Click any numbered trend pill below the category chips to open its video strip. The page shows the trend title, category, music track, number of participating creators, total views, and video count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the trend card

Each active trend shows:

- **Category badge**: the format type, shown as a color-coded chip
- **Music track**: the name of the audio used in the meme
- **Creator count**: how many accounts have joined the trend
- **Total views**: combined views across all known videos
- **Videos**: the number of videos in the current strip
- **Description**: a short explanation of the format, when available

Below the summary, you may see a **Clip breakdown** section for the top representative video. Click **Show** to expand it and view the cut-by-cut composition, including thumbnails and directions for each segment.

## Browse videos

The video grid below the summary shows the actual TikTok posts in the trend. Each video card displays the thumbnail, title, view count, like count, comment count, creator handle, and the first few hashtags. Click any video card to open a detail dialog with the clip analysis panel.

Videos load in batches as you scroll. If more videos exist, the grid continues to load automatically when you reach the bottom.

## Result

You now have a focused view of a single meme format, with verified examples, stats, and a cut breakdown. Use this to decide whether a trend fits your content strategy or campaign.
