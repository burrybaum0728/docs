The Trends page in picobean shows viral content clusters across TikTok and Instagram. You can browse trending posts, compare top-performing creators and hashtags, and narrow results by topic, audience, and timeframe. This guide explains the key features and how to filter for the insights you need.

<Note>
  The Trends and Meme Radar features require the **Trend Research** plugin. If you do not see these pages in your sidebar, turn on **Trend Research** under **Plugin Selection** in [Settings](/account/settings).
</Note>

## What you will learn

- How to browse the content grid and leaderboard views
- How to filter by platform, country, topic, and audience
- How to read trend highlights and rankings

## Prerequisites

- A picobean account with the **Trend Research** plugin enabled
- At least one topic configured (the default topic loads automatically)

## Browse content and leaderboards

The Trends page has two main views that you can switch between.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Content tab">
    Click **Content** at the top of the page to see a grid of trending posts. Each card shows the thumbnail, title, creator handle, view count, like count, and comment count. Click any card to open a detail panel with metrics and upload history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to the Trend tab">
    Click **Trend** to open a four-column leaderboard:

    - **Discover**: trending keywords and topics
    - **Theme**: top content collections grouped by theme
    - **Influencer**: creators with the highest engagement
    - **Hashtag**: hashtags that are currently viral
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a topic">
    Use the title dropdown to choose a topic. Selecting a topic scopes the entire page to that subject. The default view shows all topics.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter the results

Use the filter bar to refine the grid or leaderboard. You can combine multiple filters at once.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a time range">
    Click the date range control and select **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days**, or **All time**. The default is **Last 30 days**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add keyword or hashtag search">
    Type a keyword into the search field, or switch the field to **Hashtag** mode and enter a tag (with or without the `#`). Results update shortly after you stop typing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply facet filters">
    Use the dropdowns to filter by:

    - **Category**: the content category
    - **Brands**: brands mentioned in the content
    - **Pain point**: audience pain points
    - **Style**: content style tags
    - **Personas**: inferred creator personas
    - **Gender**: audience gender
    - **Ages**: audience age groups

    Every filter except **Category** supports multiple selections.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by follower tier">
    Use the **Followers** dropdown to show only creators in a specific size tier:

    - Nano (1K–10K)
    - Micro (10K–50K)
    - Mid-tier (50K–250K)
    - Macro (250K–1M)
    - Mega (1M+)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sort the grid">
    Choose a sort order from the dropdown:

    - **Newest**: most recently published first
    - **Most viewed**: highest total views first
    - **Fastest growing**: highest viral growth rate first
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Explore highlights and themes

The left panel shows curated highlight cards. Click a card to filter the grid to that collection. You can also expand the panel to browse theme cards. Selecting a theme narrows the grid to posts that match that brand and pain-point pair. To clear any highlight or theme filter, click the same card again.

## Result

After you apply filters, the content grid or leaderboard refreshes to show only the matching posts or rankings. The header displays a live count of how many posts match your current filters. As you scroll, more results load automatically.
